Privacy Policy — ALYTRON Music

Last updated: October 16, 2026 · Controller: ALYTRON CORPORATION S.A.C. · Privacy contact: alytroncorporationsac@gmail.com

This policy explains how the ALYTRON Music app (package com.alytron.music, "the app") handles information on your Android device. It is published by ALYTRON CORPORATION S.A.C., the developer named on the Google Play listing.

Summary

  • The app plays the music you already have on your device. It needs no account, shows no ads and includes no third‑party analytics, advertising or crash‑reporting tools.
  • Nothing the app handles is sent to servers owned by ALYTRON CORPORATION S.A.C. or by third parties. The app does not connect to Internet servers.

What information the app handles and why

Information Where it is What it is used for
Your audio library data (title, artist, album, duration, folder and cover art) Read from device storage through Android's media service; the app keeps a copy in its private storage Showing and playing your music
Favorites, playlists, recent items, queue and playback position The app's private storage Resuming where you left off
Settings (language, theme, included or excluded folders, etc.) The app's private storage Personalizing the app
Folders you pick with Android's file picker Android stores the access grant for the app Building the library from the folders you choose
Backup you export (JSON file) Wherever you choose to save it Restoring favorites, playlists and settings

What the app does not do

  • It does not create accounts or ask for personal data.
  • It does not show ads or use the advertising ID.
  • It does not use third‑party analytics, advertising or crash reporting.
  • It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, messages or calls.
  • It does not sell, rent or share information with third parties.

Permissions the app requests

Permission Why
Music and audio (READ_MEDIA_AUDIO; on Android 12 or lower, READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) Reading your music files
Notifications Showing playback controls
Foreground service (media playback) Keeping music running while the app is in the background
Network state and keep awake (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, WAKE_LOCK) Continuous and reliable playback

Security

  • The app's information lives in its private storage, protected by Android.

Retention and deletion

  • Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can clear the history and forget trusted devices from within the app. When you uninstall the app or clear its data, everything it stores is removed.
  • Backups you export stay wherever you save them, under your control.
  • Because ALYTRON CORPORATION S.A.C. does not receive your information, it holds no copy and cannot recover or delete it for you.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not collect data from anyone.

Third parties

If you install the app from Google Play, Google may collect technical information (for example, crash reports) under its own policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. ALYTRON CORPORATION S.A.C. does not control that processing.

Your rights

Because the information is processed only on your device and ALYTRON CORPORATION S.A.C. does not receive it, we hold no personal data about you to access, correct or delete. If you have a question or want to exercise a right (for example under the GDPR or Brazil's LGPD), write to alytroncorporationsac@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes how it handles information, we will publish the new version here and update the date. Important changes will also be noted in the release notes.
